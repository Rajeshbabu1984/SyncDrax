Privacy

This page describes what SyncTact stores so the product can run. It is not a sale of your data.

Account

If you sign up, we store your name, email, a hash of your password, and whether that email has been confirmed. Password reset and confirmation codes are kept only until they are used or they expire. We do not keep the password itself.

Chat and files

Messages, reactions, files, and server membership stay in the database so the people in that server can see them. Deleting a message or a server removes that content from the app.

Meetings

Video and audio travel between the people in the call. SyncTact does not record a meeting unless someone in the call presses record, and that recording stays on their device. If this server has no private relay, calls may use a public relay so people on different networks can connect. In that case the media passes through that relay. A private TURN account keeps that path on a relay you choose.

On your device

The site stores your sign-in token, your name, and the last server you opened in local storage in the browser. Signing out removes the token.

Email

If the server has an email provider configured, it can send confirmation codes, password resets, and a note when an event you are attending starts. Without a provider, those messages are not sent.
